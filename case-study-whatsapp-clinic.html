FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before deploying the booking agent for your clinic.

Still have questions? Book a call →
Does the patient need to install anything?
▼
No. Patients use their existing WhatsApp no app download, no login, no form to fill. They just send a message and the AI handles the rest.
What if a patient asks something the AI can't answer?
▼
The system detects ambiguous or out-of-scope queries and automatically flags them for a human staff member to follow up no patient is left without a response.
How does it check which slots are available?
▼
The agent reads your clinic's live Google Sheets calendar in real time. When a slot is booked, it's logged instantly so double-booking is impossible. You can update the calendar yourself at any time.
Can patients message in Hindi or regional languages?
▼
Yes. The AI detects the patient's language and responds in the same language automatically supporting English, Malay, Mandarin, Tamil and more with no additional configuration.
Can this work for multiple doctors or departments?
▼
Absolutely. The system handles multiple doctors, departments and time-slot rules. Patients can specify who they want to see and the AI routes accordingly.
