FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up a custom, self-hosted outreach tracking system.

Still have questions? Let's connect →
Why shouldn't I just use standard email software?
▼
Standard platforms charge monthly fees per seat and route messages through shared servers, increasing spam risks. Our setup runs directly from your business domain, has $0/month seat costs, and delivers straight to the primary inbox.
How does the filter block automated bot opens?
▼
Spam filters load links instantly to check for malware. Our system checks the time gap between send and open; triggers under 2 minutes are identified as security checks and ignored, giving you clean data.
Can I send follow-ups in the same email thread?
▼
Yes. Subsequent steps link directly to the initial email thread ID. Follow-ups reply under your first message, creating a natural conversation layout.
Do I need to manage servers or write code?
▼
No. We handle the entire custom build and integration. Once live, your team controls contacts and monitors campaigns directly inside a Google Sheet.
