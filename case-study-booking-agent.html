Before & After

The same clinic, salon, or consultancy — completely different operation. Manual booking chaos on the left, AI-handled 24/7 booking on the right.

Before — Manual Booking
  • Receptionist answers calls during office hours only — after-hours calls go to voicemail
  • Patients WhatsApp at night and wait until morning for a reply
  • Booking emails sit unread until someone checks the inbox
  • Double bookings happen because staff update three different systems manually
  • Cancellations create last-minute gaps that go unfilled
  • Staff spend 60% of their time managing the calendar, not delivering service
After — AI Booking Agent
  • Patients book any time — WhatsApp, call, or email — confirmed in seconds
  • All channels share one database — no double bookings possible
  • Automated reminders sent 24h and 30 min before — no-shows drop 60%
  • Cancellations trigger automatic re-marketing to waitlist contacts
  • Staff see a clean, filled calendar every morning
  • No receptionist needed for routine booking — only real human interaction

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before deploying an AI booking agent for your business.

Still have questions? Book a call →
Does this work for any type of service business?
▼
Yes — clinics, dental practices, salons, gyms, consultancies, pest control, physiotherapy, tutoring, and any business that books time-based appointments. The AI is configured around your specific slots, staff, and cancellation rules.
Do I need to run all three channels?
▼
No — you pick the channels your customers actually use. Most businesses start with WhatsApp only, then add Voice or Email later. All channels share the same booking database, so there's no double-booking risk regardless of how many you run.
How does the Voice channel handle complex situations?
▼
The Voice AI handles 90%+ of calls: booking, rescheduling, cancellations, answering FAQs about location, pricing, and availability. For genuinely complex situations it says "Let me transfer you to a team member" and hands off gracefully. You define what triggers a handoff.
What calendar or booking system does it connect to?
▼
The system connects to Google Calendar, or we set up a PostgreSQL-backed booking database (available as an add-on). Either way, all channels sync to the same source of truth — no double bookings.
Is this a subscription or a one-time build?
▼
One-time custom build. You own the system. There are small ongoing costs for third-party APIs (WhatsApp Business API, Voice API, AI model calls) but there's no Karlspace subscription fee. We discuss all running costs upfront before you commit to anything.
