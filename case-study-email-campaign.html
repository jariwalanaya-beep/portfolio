The System

From one text to sent emails in seconds

Three steps from typing in Telegram to emails landing in inboxes. No setup per campaign. No manual work. Just speak naturally.

01
💬
You Type in Telegram

Send a plain-text message like "Send 20% discount email for Product X to all buyers." No special syntax, no form to fill just speak naturally.

02
🧠
AI Reads Intent & Queries DB

The system extracts the product ID, identifies the audience type (customers vs suppliers), and fetches only the relevant contacts from your database nobody else gets included.

03
🚀
Emails Written & Sent

An LLM writes a context-aware subject line and body promotional tone for customers, B2B tone for suppliers then fires every email through Gmail instantly. Done.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before we build the email automation for your business.

Still have questions? Book a call →
What database does this work with?
▼
It works with your existing data source Google Sheets, Airtable, a SQL database, or any structured data format. We connect the AI to wherever your customer and supplier records already live. No migration needed.
Can I customise the email tone and style?
▼
Yes. During setup, you provide a few example emails that represent your brand voice. The AI learns from these and replicates your style including your typical greeting, sign-off, and level of formality in every campaign it writes.
Does this send from my actual Gmail or a third-party address?
▼
Your own Gmail account, using the official Gmail API. Emails show your real sender address and display name building trust and avoiding spam filters that third-party bulk senders often trigger.
What if I mis-type a message or make an error?
▼
The system confirms the campaign details back to you in Telegram before sending showing you the product, audience count, and a preview of the email copy. You approve with a single message. Nothing goes out without your explicit confirmation.
How is this different from just using Mailchimp?
▼
Mailchimp is built for marketing teams who have time to log in, build segments, write copy, and set up campaigns. This is built for owners who want to send a targeted campaign from their phone in under 60 seconds while also notifying suppliers in the same action.
