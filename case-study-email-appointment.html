FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before deploying the email viewing agent for your agency.

Still have questions? Email Shivam →
Does the property lead need to use any special format in their email?
▼
No. Leads write naturally. The AI extraction agent parses free-form email text and pulls out structured data (name, phone, property interest, budget, preferred date/time) automatically — no templates or forms.
What happens if a lead requests a time when all agents are busy?
▼
The system detects the conflict, informs the lead politely, and suggests the next 3 available slots across all agents — keeping the momentum going without staff involvement.
How does the AI classify real estate emails accurately?
▼
The classifier AI reads the email and decides the intent — viewing request, cancellation, or general query. If it's a viewing request, the extractor AI pulls structured JSON (name, property interest, budget, preferred time). Separating these tasks boosts accuracy to 98%+.
Can leads cancel or reschedule a viewing by email?
▼
Yes. Patients email their unique appointment code to cancel or reschedule. The system verifies the code, updates the PostgreSQL database, releases the slot, and confirms the change — all automatically.
Can this work across multiple agents and property types?
▼
Absolutely. The system reads from an agents table in PostgreSQL — listing all available agents and their schedules. Leads pick their preferred agent or property type, and the AI handles routing and availability checks per agent automatically.
What if a lead's email is missing budget or timing details?
▼
The extraction agent identifies exactly which fields are missing (name, budget, preferred area, date, etc.) and sends a targeted follow-up asking only for the missing information — not a generic "please provide all details" message.
