FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before we build your dashboard.

Still have questions? Book a call →
Does my Google Sheet need to be formatted a specific way?
▼
No. We work with your Sheet as-is. Whether it's a simple sales log, a complex inventory tracker, or a multi-tab financial model — we map it into your dashboard. You don't need to change anything.
Can my whole team see the dashboard?
▼
Yes. You can share a live, read-only link with your team (or specific people). They can view and interact with the dashboard from any device — phone, tablet, desktop — without needing to access the raw Sheet.
What happens if I change my Sheet structure later?
▼
Minor changes — new rows, renamed columns — are handled automatically. If you significantly restructure the Sheet (add new tabs, change core columns), we'll update the dashboard mapping for you, usually within 24 hours.
Is my business data safe?
▼
The dashboard connects to your Sheet using read-only access via Google's secure API. We never store a copy of your data — the dashboard reads directly from your Sheet. Your data stays in your Google account at all times.
What types of charts and KPIs can you build?
▼
Line charts, bar charts, pie charts, tables, scorecards, trend comparisons, and anomaly alerts. We also set up key KPI tiles for things like revenue, expenses, units sold, fulfilment rate — whatever your business tracks most.
